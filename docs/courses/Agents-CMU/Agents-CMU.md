# index {#index}


!!! quote "Course Overview"
    This course studies LLM-based AI agents: systems that use large language models to 
    perceive, reason, plan, and act over many steps. Agents interact with their environments — calling tools, navigating interfaces, writing code, working alongside people — to complete real tasks.

    The course covers both the foundations of building agents and active research questions around long-horizon reasoning, training, safety, and interaction. We emphasize hands-on implementation with modern frameworks and the training methods that make agents more capable and reliable.


这门课希望我们最终能做到的事情：
1. 在open-source LLM上从零实现agent。
2. 为multi-step tasks设计evaluation。
3. 训练agent，改善它的能力。
4. 分析safety和reliability之间的trade-offs。
5. 探索一个与agents有关的开放研究问题。
